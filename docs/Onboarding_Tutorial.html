ABM Discovery, your getting started guide

Hi Galyna, welcome aboard. This is going to be your new home base. Give me ten minutes and by the end of this page you'll know exactly what to do each morning, and why it works. No rush, and nothing here is breakable, so poke around as you read.

So what is this thing, really

Think of it as a research assistant that never sleeps. Every weekday it goes out and looks for US healthcare companies that are quietly showing signs they might need what Magical sells. Things like a hospital posting a pile of billing and prior auth jobs, a new finance leader stepping in, a funding round, a layoff, or someone engaging with our posts.

Then it does the part you'll love. It ranks them, so the companies that look most ready are sitting right at the top when you log in. You are not hunting through noise. You are walking into a tidy list that already did the sorting for you.

One word you'll see a lot is signal. A signal is just a reason a company showed up. A job posting, a leadership change, a funding round. That's all it means.

The four tabs, in plain words

Across the top you'll see four places to go. Here is the whole map on one screen, then we'll spend most of our time in the first one.

Discovery is the inbox of new opportunities. Watch list is where the low priority ones wait so your inbox stays clean. Scored is your shortlist of researched accounts. News gives you talking points. Simple as that.

Your morning, one row at a time

Open Discovery. You'll see a list of companies, the most promising ones first. Each row tells you everything you need at a glance. Here's one, with the parts that matter pointed out.

1

Read the name and the little tags. The ABM target tag means this company is on your target sheet, so it matters to you no matter what.

2

Glance at the score on the right. Higher means they're showing more signs of being in the market. A Hot tag means they're worth your attention today.

3

Like what you see? Hit Score. That sends the company off to be researched in depth, and it moves over to your Scored tab. That's the whole action. One click.

What the score is actually telling you

The number is not a guess about whether they're a good fit. It's about timing, how in the market they look right now, based on the signals. A brand new finance leader, a few open billing roles at once, a layoff, those push the score up. A single quiet job posting keeps it low.

You don't have to memorize any of this. The system already did the math, and there's a small How intent is scored link in the panel if you ever get curious. The short version, trust the order. The top of the list is where your time is best spent.

Let it score the hot ones for you

Here's a lovely shortcut. You don't have to click Score on every hot company yourself. There's a switch up top called Auto-score. Flip it on, and once a day it quietly sends the genuinely hot ones off for research on its own. You'll see them waiting in Scored when you check.

Good to know, Auto-score only ever touches the hot ones, and it always respects your monthly budget. It will never run up a surprise bill. More on that near the end.

The Watch list, where the quiet ones wait

Not every company is ready today. A hospital with a single routine billing opening is worth keeping an eye on, but it's not worth your time or a research spend yet. Those land in the Watch list instead of cluttering your Discovery view.

Here's the clever bit. They don't just sit there forever. If one of them heats up, say a second role opens or a real signal lands, it pops right back into Discovery on its own. And each one shows a little timer so you can see when it'll quietly age out if nothing changes.

You mostly won't need to touch this tab. It just keeps Discovery focused on the companies actually worth your day.

Scored, your shortlist

This is where the good stuff collects. Every company you scored, plus the ones Auto-score sent over, show up here fully researched. You get a fit picture, the reasons behind it, and the signals that brought them in. This is your call sheet.

When you hit Export, you get a clean spreadsheet. And it now carries a Signals column, so the reasons each account showed up travel with it. No more wondering later why a company is on the list.

Bringing in your own list

Already have an ABM tracking sheet? Lovely, bring it in. Upload it and the system is smart about it. If some of those companies are already sitting in Discovery, it doesn't make a messy duplicate. It quietly pulls them over into Scored, signals and all, and tidies them out of Discovery. The ones it's never seen come in fresh.

They land as ready to score, not scored, so a big upload never spends money on its own. You score them in a batch when you're ready.

Two more places to peek

News

The News tab pulls healthcare headlines that are relevant to what we sell, things like new prior auth rules or denial trends. Each one comes with a little note on why it matters and a ready made angle. Perfect for the first line of an email when you want a reason to reach out.

Social listening

Behind the scenes, the system also watches LinkedIn. It spots decision makers engaging with our posts, or with competitors, or chatting about industry events. Those quietly become signals too. You don't have to do anything, it just makes the list smarter over time.

About the money, so you never worry

Researching a company costs a little, so there's a budget built in, and a single meter at the top shows everything in one place. No hunting around for what cost what.

The number resets on its own at the start of each month. If you ever bump the ceiling, the system just pauses new research until next month or until the budget is raised. Truly nothing to babysit.

Your daily ten minutes

That's everything. Here's the whole routine, the only list you need to remember.

  1. Open Discovery. Skim from the top, the best are already there.
  2. For the ones you like, click Score. Or just leave Auto-score on and let the hot ones go themselves.
  3. Pop over to Scored to see what's been researched, and Export when you're ready to reach out.
  4. Grab a hook from News if you want a reason to start the conversation.
  5. Ignore the Watch list and the spend meter unless you're curious. They look after themselves.

You've got this. It was built to make your day simpler, not busier. Welcome again.
